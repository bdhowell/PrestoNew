Within the context of this application may be PNGs or JPGs; Latex or ASCII Math rendered with MathJax; or charts rendered with Chart.js.
Graphics are inserted into section text and can snap to eight locations within the section:

1. top-left, 
2. top-center, 
3. top-right,
4. left-middle,
5. right-middle,
6. bottom-left, 
7. bottom-center,
8. bottom-right

If a graphic is not snapped to one of those eight points, then it is free-floating within the text and may be cut, copied, or pasted.
If a graphic is between two paragraphs and not snapped then the graphic is implicitly centered.
